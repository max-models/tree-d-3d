tree-d-3d stands for *Tree Dynamics in 3D*. For now the particles move in a plane, with the 3D
Coulomb field of point charges or the 2D field of line charges. Full 3D, on an octree, is planned.

## What is included

- **Flat quadtree.** Point charges (3D Coulomb) or line charges (2D Poisson) at any targets, by Barnes-Hut tree walk or direct sum.
- **Fast multipole method.** O(N) fields of line charges with a dual-tree traversal and complex multipole expansions.
- **Simulation.** Second-order time integration with a uniform magnetic field, absorbing walls and objects, and cached fields.
- **Electrodes.** Conductors at prescribed potentials by the panel method, built from any 2D simgeometry shape.
- **Kernels.** Every kernel in Pyccel, NumPy, CUDA and Metal, with the same arguments.
